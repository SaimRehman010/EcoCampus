# EcoCampus Sustainability & Operations Policy Manual

Welcome to the official **EcoCampus Sustainability Guidelines and Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)**. All faculty, students, staff, and facility managers are required to comply with these environmental protocols.

---

## 1. HVAC & Climate Control Policy

### 1.1 Operating Schedule & Setpoints
- **Standard Cooling Setpoint:** All campus air conditioning and HVAC units must remain set to **24°C (75°F)** during warm months. Setting thermostats below 23°C is strictly prohibited.
- **Operating Hours:** Central HVAC operates Monday through Friday from **07:30 AM to 06:30 PM**. On weekends, HVAC is only provided for pre-booked administrative events.
- **Night-time & Non-Operational Shutdown:** All localized split AC units must be powered off when leaving rooms. Security personnel will conduct sweeps at 08:00 PM and issue violation notices for running ACs in empty rooms.

### 1.2 Specialized Facility Exceptions (Computer Labs & Server Rooms)
- **Computer Labs (CS & Data Centers):** Thermostats are regulated between **21°C - 23°C** to prevent server and GPU overheating. Lab doors and windows must remain closed at all times while AC is active.
- **After-Hours Lab Usage:** After 06:30 PM, students remaining in labs must use designated "Zone A" clustered workstations to prevent cooling unoccupied rooms.
- **Automated Scheduling:** Computer labs are equipped with PIR smart motion and temperature sensors. If no motion is detected for 20 minutes, the AC switches to low-power eco-mode (26°C).

## 2. Water Conservation & Leak Escalation Protocol

### 2.1 Escalation Tiers & Response Times
When a water leak, fixture defect, or overflow is identified, it must be reported through the EcoCampus portal or emergency dispatch immediately.

| Severity Tier | Description | SLA / Response Time | Action Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1 (Minor)** | Slow faucet drip, running toilet tank, damp ceiling tile | Within 24 hours | Submit regular report on EcoCampus portal. Facilities team will dispatch technician. |
| **Tier 2 (Moderate)** | Broken valve, steady stream from pipe joint, puddle forming on floor | Within 2 hours | Log as High Priority on portal and notify building floor warden. |
| **Tier 3 (Critical / Emergency)** | Major pipe burst, ceiling collapse, basement pump room flooding | Immediate (< 15 mins) | Call Campus Emergency Dispatch (ext. 9110 / 555-0199) AND submit urgent report. Main riser shutoff executed immediately. |

### 2.2 Restroom & Irrigation Rules
- Automated dual-flush sensors in restrooms must be inspected bi-weekly.
- Campus landscape irrigation operates exclusively during early morning (05:00 AM - 06:30 AM) using recycled greywater. Overhead sprayers during daylight hours are prohibited.

## 3. Lighting Schedules & Smart Controls

### 3.1 Lecture Halls & Classrooms
- **Occupancy Sensors:** Classrooms are fitted with passive infrared (PIR) motion sensors. Lights automatically turn off after **15 minutes of zero motion**.
- **Daylight Harvesting:** Fixtures within 4 meters of windows must remain dimmed by 50% during sunny hours (10:00 AM - 04:00 PM) to utilize natural daylight.
- **Manual Overrides:** The last occupant leaving a room must manually turn off switches regardless of sensor timers.

### 3.2 Corridors & Perimeter Security
- Main campus corridors reduce to 30% ambient LED lighting after **09:00 PM**.
- Outdoor perimeter walkways utilize solar-assisted smart LEDs that automatically activate at sunset and dim to 40% at midnight.

## 4. Waste Management & Recycling Segregation

### 4.1 Four-Stream Color-Coded Bins
EcoCampus enforces source segregation across all buildings:
- **Blue Bin (Paper & Cardboard):** Clean notebooks, printer paper, cardboard cartons, clean flyers. *(No greasy pizza boxes or contaminated tissues).*
- **Yellow Bin (Plastics, Metals & Glass):** Clean plastic bottles, soda cans, clean aluminum foil, glass bottles.
- **Green Bin (Organic & Food Waste):** Fruit peels, leftover food, tea bags, coffee grounds. Food waste is diverted to the campus organic composting facility.
- **Red Bin / Hazardous Collection (Chemical & Biohazard):** Lab reagents, biological samples, gloves, sharps. Handled strictly by certified hazardous waste operators.

### 4.2 Electronic Waste (E-Waste) & Battery Disposal
- Old laptops, motherboards, circuit boards, batteries, and charging cables must **never** be discarded in municipal trash.
- Dedicated E-Waste bins are situated at:
  1. Engineering Building - Ground Floor Lobby
  2. Student Union - Next to the Helpdesk
  3. IT Department - Server Room 102
